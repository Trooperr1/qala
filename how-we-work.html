Testing & Revisions

Before launch, we thoroughly test everything and gather your feedback. Your package includes 2-3 revision rounds to ensure every detail is exactly as you want it. This isn't about "fixing bugs"—it's about making it perfect.

Our Testing Checklist:

  • ✓ Cross-browser compatibility (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge)
  • ✓ Mobile responsiveness on all screen sizes
  • ✓ Page load speed optimization (<3 seconds)
  • ✓ Form submissions and integrations
  • ✓ SEO basics (meta tags, sitemaps, robots.txt)
  • ✓ Security best practices (HTTPS, input validation)
  • ✓ Accessibility standards (WCAG 2.1)

OUR VALUES

The Principles That Guide Us

These aren't just buzzwords—they're how we actually work

🎯 Results Over Activity

We don't bill hourly because we don't reward busy work. We care about one thing: did it work? If your chatbot doesn't reduce support tickets or your website doesn't generate leads, we haven't done our job.

💬 Radical Transparency

No jargon, no excuses, no surprises. If something goes wrong, we tell you immediately and explain how we'll fix it. Our proposals show exactly what you're paying for, line by line.

⚡ Speed Without Shortcuts

Fast doesn't mean sloppy. We move quickly by avoiding unnecessary meetings, bureaucracy, and over-engineering. But we never compromise on security, performance, or code quality.

🛡️ Your Success = Our Success

We don't do projects and disappear. Many of our best clients started with one small project and now work with us on multiple initiatives because we actually care about their growth.

🔧 Technology as a Tool

We use AI, modern frameworks, and cutting-edge tools—but only when they actually improve the outcome. We don't chase trends or use fancy tech just to look impressive.

🤝 True Partnership

You're not just a client—you're a partner. We'll tell you when your idea won't work, suggest better approaches, and sometimes push back if we believe there's a smarter path forward.
