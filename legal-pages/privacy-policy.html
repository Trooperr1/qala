3. Information We Collect

3.1 Information You Provide to Us:

  • Contact information (name, email, phone number, company name)

4. How We Use Your Information

We use your information for the following purposes:

  • Service Delivery: To provide our web development, AI solutions, and social media management services
  • Communication: To respond to inquiries, send updates about your projects, and provide customer support
  • Improvement: To analyze website usage and improve our services
  • Legal Compliance: To comply with applicable laws and regulations
  • Security: To protect against fraud and ensure the security of our systems

5. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR/nFADP)

We process your personal data based on:

  • Consent: When you provide explicit consent (e.g., contact form submissions)
  • Contract Performance: To fulfill our contractual obligations to you
  • Legitimate Interest: For website analytics and service improvements
  • Legal Obligation: When required by Swiss or EU law

6. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We may share your information with:

  • Service Providers: Third-party vendors who assist with hosting, email delivery (FormSubmit), analytics, and payment processing
  • Google Translate: For multi-language support (see Google's Privacy Policy)
  • Legal Requirements: When required by law or to protect our rights

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third parties.

7. Data Retention

We retain your personal data only as long as necessary for the purposes outlined in this policy or as required by law. Typically:

  • Contact form submissions: 3 years
  • Client project data: Duration of engagement + 10 years (Swiss commercial law)
  • Website analytics: 14 months

8. Your Rights Under Swiss nFADP

You have the following rights:

  • Access: Request a copy of your personal data
  • Rectification: Correct inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Erasure: Request deletion of your data ("right to be forgotten")
  • Data Portability: Receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format
  • Restriction: Limit how we process your data
  • Objection: Object to processing based on legitimate interests
  • Withdraw Consent: Withdraw consent at any time

To exercise these rights, contact us at [your-email@example.com]. We will respond within 30 days.

9. International Data Transfers

Your data may be transferred to and processed in countries outside Switzerland. We ensure adequate protection through standard contractual clauses approved by the Swiss Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC).

10. Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your data, including encryption (HTTPS), secure servers, and access controls. However, no internet transmission is 100% secure.

11. Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed to individuals under 16. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If we learn we have collected such data, we will delete it promptly.
