Cookie Policy

Last updated: January 2025

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device (computer, smartphone, tablet) when you visit a website. They help websites remember your preferences, improve functionality, and analyze traffic.

2. How We Use Cookies

JAFF STUDIO uses cookies to enhance your browsing experience, analyze website performance, and provide personalized content. We respect your privacy and comply with Swiss and EU cookie regulations.

3. Types of Cookies We Use

3.1 Essential Cookies (Strictly Necessary)

These cookies are required for basic website functionality and cannot be disabled.

Cookie Name Purpose Duration
session_id Maintains your session across pages Session
language_preference Remembers your language choice 1 year

3.2 Functional Cookies

These cookies enhance functionality and personalization.

Cookie Name Purpose Duration
googtrans Google Translate language preference Session

3.3 Analytics Cookies

These cookies help us understand how visitors use our website (page views, traffic sources, time on site).

Note: We currently do not use analytics cookies. If we add analytics in the future (e.g., Google Analytics), we will update this policy and request your consent where required.

3.4 Marketing Cookies (Not Currently Used)

We do not currently use marketing or advertising cookies. If this changes, we will notify you and request consent.

4. Third-Party Cookies

Some cookies are set by third-party services we use:

  • Google Translate: Sets cookies to remember your language preference. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • CDN Services (Three.js, GSAP): External scripts may set minimal cookies for functionality.

5. Cookie Duration

  • Session Cookies: Deleted when you close your browser
  • Persistent Cookies: Remain on your device until expiration or manual deletion

6. How to Manage Cookies

You have control over cookies. Here's how to manage them:

6.1 Browser Settings

Most browsers allow you to:

  • View cookies stored on your device
  • Delete all or specific cookies
  • Block cookies from specific sites
  • Block all third-party cookies
  • Clear cookies when closing the browser

Browser-Specific Instructions:

  • Chrome: Settings → Privacy and security → Cookies and other site data
  • Firefox: Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data
  • Safari: Preferences → Privacy → Manage Website Data
  • Edge: Settings → Privacy, search, and services → Cookies and site permissions

6.2 Impact of Blocking Cookies

Blocking essential cookies may affect website functionality. You may experience issues with language preferences, navigation, or form submissions.

7. Do Not Track (DNT)

Our website respects Do Not Track browser signals. When DNT is enabled, we minimize non-essential tracking.

We may update this Cookie Policy to reflect changes in technology, regulations, or our practices. The "Last updated" date indicates when changes were made.
